Scopion is installed

This spreadsheet was open before Scopion was installed. Reload the tab, then press Ctrl+Shift+A.

It shows which cells a formula reads, and walks you through them. Three steps to the first trace:

  1. Open a Google Sheets spreadsheet (reload the tab if it was already open).
  2. Select a cell that contains a formula.
  3. Press Ctrl+Shift+A, or click the Scopion icon in the toolbar.

The Google sign-in prompt, explained

The first trace asks you to sign in and grant access to your spreadsheets. Scopion reads the sheet through the Google Sheets API to show values, named ranges and hidden sheets. It makes exactly one kind of change: when the walk enters a hidden sheet, Sheets unhides it, and Scopion hides it again when you close the panel. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google's own API.

Keys

↑ ↓
walk the references; the selection follows
→
make the highlighted cell the new origin
← / Backspace
go back one origin
Enter
close and stay where the walk left you
Esc / OK
return to where you started and close

Good to know

  • Excel files opened in Sheets (.xlsx) have no API data: references and jumps work, values show as —.
  • OFFSET, INDEX and INDIRECT are resolved from literals and single-cell values; anything else is listed as unresolved, never dropped.
  • If Ctrl+Shift+A is taken by another extension, rebind it in Chrome's shortcut settings.
